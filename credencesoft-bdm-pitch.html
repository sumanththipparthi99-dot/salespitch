Inventory Mismatch

The Problem

Manual inventory updates every 6 hours create gaps between what's available on your PMS and what's showing on Booking.com, Airbnb, and your website.

Real Consequences

A guest books on Booking.com for a specific room type. Front desk hasn't synced yet—room is already sold on your direct website. Guest arrives. Room unavailable. Complaint. Refund. Bad review.

BookOne Solution

One-second synchronization across all channels. When a room is booked anywhere, it's instantly unavailable everywhere. Zero overbookings. Zero confusion.

Delayed Check-ins

The Problem

18-minute average check-in during peak hours because front desk staff must manually:

  • Verify payment status across 3 systems
  • Check housekeeping readiness (via phone/radio)
  • Manually enter guest details in 2+ systems
  • Process payment (if not pre-captured)

Guest Experience Impact

After traveling for 6+ hours, your guest waits 18 minutes in line. They're already in a bad mood. First impression of hotel is chaos.

BookOne Solution

Integrated PMS + HotelMate app enables:

  • Digital check-in via app (room key sent to phone)
  • Payment pre-captured at booking
  • Automatic housekeeping coordination
  • Welcome message + room instructions

Result: 2.3-minute check-in. Guest feels valued. Premium experience.
